About Glowpower FZ LLC

GloPower FZ LLC is a leading power solutions company, delivering comprehensive generator systems ranging from 9 kVA to 4000 kVA for diesel gensets and 17 kVA to 3125 kVA for gas gensets. The company executes large infrastructure power projects from concept design, detailed engineering to installation and commissioning.

Backed by factory-trained service team, GloPower provides prompt and professional product Support including AMC, O&M Contracts and all levels of repair services. The company’s customer-friendly pricing with quick availability of genuine spare parts, ensures uninterrupted operations and lasting value for clients.

Message from the Management

Looking back at the journey of GloPower FZ LLC, I feel a deep sense of pride and gratitude. Established in 2024 within the UAE Special Economic Zone, GloPower was built with a crystal clear goal of extending our group's legacy of reliable power solutions across the Middle East and Africa. With over three decades of experience in the power generation industry — including collaborations with renowned names like Perkins, MTU, Volvo, Generac and Baudouin — I have learned that real success is built on trust, long-term relationships, and a genuine dedication to solving our customers' challenges. GloPower embodies these values, with project teams and product support specialists working tirelessly to deliver high-quality generator solutions and ensure our clients always feel supported, no matter how complex the project or how remote the location.

With a strong foundation in India through Uila Power Pvt. Ltd. and our steadfast operation in Gabon through Samba Tech & Co., our aim has always been to synthesize local expertise with global reach. I am deeply grateful to our patrons, partners, and team members who have placed their faith in us and contributed to our journey so far.

Together, we are not just powering industries and infrastructure — we are lighting the way for progress and opportunity across continents, and I look forward with confidence and excitement to everything we will achieve together in the coming years.

Atul Jain - Chairman GloPower FZ LLC
