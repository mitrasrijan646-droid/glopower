Electrical Equipment

Engineered Power Distribution, Built for Reliability

Complete electrical infrastructure — from switchgear to transformers — engineered for safe, seamless power delivery across critical and industrial sites.

Three Critical Systems

One Integrated Power Chain

Electrical Panels:

Control, Protect, Automate

From MV/HV switchgear to LT panels, our panels manage distribution, protection, and synchronization across your power network.

  • Medium & High Voltage switchgear for primary distribution
  • LT panels for final-circuit power management
  • Generator synchronizing panels for seamless multi-set operation
  • Automatic Transfer Switches (ATS) for uninterrupted backup transition
  • PLC & automation panels for remote monitoring and control

Cables & Cable Trays:

The Power Backbone

Engineered cabling systems that safely carry power and signal across every voltage tier and installation environment.

  • HV and LV power cables for primary and secondary distribution
  • Shielded / screened cables for signal integrity in high-EMI zones
  • Ladder and perforated cable trays for organized, accessible routing
  • Designed for industrial, commercial and outdoor environments

Transformers:

Reliable Voltage Conversion

Oil-filled and dry-type transformers selected to match site conditions, load profiles, and safety requirements

  • Oil-filled transformers for high-capacity, outdoor installations
  • Dry-type transformers for indoor, fire-sensitive and compact spaces
  • Custom kVA ratings to match generator and grid-tie configurations
