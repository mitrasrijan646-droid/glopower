Electrical Equipment

Engineered Power Distribution, Built for Reliability

Complete electrical infrastructure — from switchgear to transformers — engineered for safe, seamless power delivery across critical and industrial sites.

Three Critical Systems

One Integrated Power Chain

Electrical Panels:

Control, Protect, Automate

MV/HV to LT panels managing distribution and automation.

  • MV/HV switchgear for primary distribution
  • LT panels for final-circuit power
  • Sync panels for multi-genset paralleling
  • ATS panels for backup transfer
  • PLC panels for remote control

Transformers:

Reliable Voltage Conversion

Oil-filled and dry-type units matched to site needs.

  • Oil-filled for high-capacity, outdoor use
  • Dry-type for indoor, fire-sensitive sites
  • Custom kVA to match load

Cables & Cable Trays:

The Power Backbone

Engineered cabling and routing for every voltage tier.

  • HV and LV power cables
  • Shielded cables for high-EMI zones
  • Ladder and perforated cable trays
  • Designed for industrial and outdoor use

Control & Monitoring:

Precision System Oversight

Devices giving operators visibility and command over performance.

  • Digital meters and monitoring instruments
  • Control relays and timers
  • HMI and SCADA interfaces
  • Selector switches and pilot lights
  • Load, temperature & fault sensors

Protection & Connection:

Safeguarding Every Connection

Protective and termination hardware specified per project.

  • Circuit breakers and fuses
  • Earth leakage and RCDs
  • Surge protection devices
  • Cable lugs, glands, terminal blocks
  • Grounding, bonding & LOTO hardware
