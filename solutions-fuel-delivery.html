Fuel Delivery Solutions
Built for Fuel Integrity

Generator performance is only as reliable as the fuel feeding it. GloPower delivers complete fuel infrastructure — from bulk storage and transfer to continuous polishing — keeping fuel clean, available, and ready across the full operating life of a site.

Bulk Fuel Storage Tanks

Above-ground and bunkered fuel storage systems sized to site demand, built to international safety and containment standards.

Fuel Transfer and Delivery Systems

Pumping, piping, and transfer infrastructure that moves fuel safely between storage, day tanks, and generators — including remote and multi-unit configurations.

Centrifuge
Systems

High-speed centrifugal separation that removes water and particulate contamination from fuel before it ever reaches the engine.

Fuel Polishing
Systems

Continuous, in-tank fuel conditioning that controls microbial growth, sediment buildup, and water ingress — extending fuel life during long-term storage.

What Bad Fuel Costs You

Diesel degrades. Water condenses inside tanks. Microbial growth thrives in stored fuel left untreated.

Left unmanaged, these are the leading causes of unplanned generator failure — not engine faults, but fuel faults.

GloPower's fuel systems are designed around this reality, treating fuel quality as an ongoing operational requirement, not a one-time delivery.

How Fuel Polishing
Works

Continuous Protection,
Not a One-Time Fix

Fuel polishing isn't a single intervention — it's a continuous cycle that keeps stored fuel within usable specification for as long as it sits in the tank.

Extract

Fuel is drawn from the storage tank through a closed-loop system

Separate & Filter

Centrifuge and multi-stage filtration remove water, sediment, and microbial contamination

Return

Conditioned fuel is returned to the tank, with the cycle repeating on a scheduled or continuous basis
