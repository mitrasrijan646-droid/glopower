Greater Noida, India

Key Project Highlights

  • Project: Balance of Plant Works for buyback and replacement of existing 4 x 2910 @11kV MTU Diesel generators with 3 x 2.5 MWe MTU Gas Generators and all related works.

Includes supply, installation, testing, and commissioning of genset accessories, gas supply and exhaust systems, electrical panels (integrated with existing systems), civil foundations, cooling and earthing systems, and control/power cabling.
