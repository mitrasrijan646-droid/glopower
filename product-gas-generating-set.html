Gas Generating Set

Clean, Reliable Power for Critical Operations

Delivering dependable prime and standby power with industry-leading engine technology

Two Proven Technologies.

One Reliable Power Solution

Rich-Burn Technology: Diesel-Like Response, Cleaner Operation

Designed for standby and backup power, rich-burn gas generators deliver:

  • Fast transient response and high load acceptance, comparable to diesel gensets.
  • Lower operating costs (OPEX).
  • Ideal for critical facilities requiring immediate power restoration, such as hospitals, data centers, and commercial buildings.

Lean-Burn Technology: Continuous Power, Maximum Efficiency

For base-load and continuous power applications, lean-burn gas generators offer:

  • Ultra-low OPEX and extended maintenance intervals.
  • Optimized fuel-air mix for high thermal efficiency and low emissions.
  • Perfect for industrial operations, power plants and Independent Power Producers (IPPs) seeking sustainable, long-term energy solutions.
