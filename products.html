The Hallmark of Efficiency

MTU delivers advanced engine solutions across both diesel and gas fuel platforms, enabling GloPower to offer versatile, future-ready power systems tailored to diverse operational needs. MTU's diesel engines are renowned for their robust performance, durability, and rapid response in mission-critical applications, while their gas engines provide cleaner, efficient, and sustainable energy generation with lower emissions and optimized lifecycle costs. Together, this dual-fuel capability empowers GloPower to support clients with reliable, high-performance power solutions that balance strength, efficiency, and environmental responsibility across industries and applications.

Precision Engineering, Localized

FMTU brings MTU's proven Series 1600 engineering to a dedicated diesel platform, combining full-authority CRDI fuel systems with industry-leading load-factor performance across the mid-to-high kVA range. Built on decades of German engine design and refined through localized manufacturing, FMTU gensets deliver near-linear fuel efficiency from partial to full load, exceptional block-loading capability, and extended maintenance intervals that reduce downtime and total cost of ownership. For GloPower, FMTU offers clients the rigor of MTU-grade engineering with the responsiveness of a regionally optimized supply chain — a dependable choice for data centres, large industrial sites, and other demanding standby or prime power applications.

Power Engineered for Endurance

Perkins has spent decades engineering diesel powertrains built for resilience in the most demanding operating environments, from remote industrial sites to continuous-duty commercial installations. Its engines are recognized for rugged simplicity, ease of maintenance, and dependable performance under sustained heavy loads, making them a trusted choice across construction, mining, and infrastructure-driven markets. For GloPower, Perkins represents accessible reliability — engines that keep running where conditions are toughest and downtime is least tolerable — giving clients a genset platform that prioritizes durability, serviceability, and long-term operating value.

Scandinavian Reliability, Engineered to Last

Volvo Penta brings a heritage of marine and industrial engine design into the diesel genset space, prioritizing low vibration, quiet operation, and consistent performance across varying load conditions. Known for clean combustion, fuel efficiency, and engineering precision rooted in Swedish manufacturing standards, Volvo Penta engines are built to perform reliably in environments where noise control and emissions compliance matter as much as raw power. For GloPower, Volvo Penta extends the product range to clients who need a refined, low-impact power solution — combining Scandinavian engineering discipline with the dependability required for sensitive or regulated installations.

Heritage Power, Modern Performance

Baudouin combines over a century of French engine-making heritage with modern diesel technology, producing engines known for smooth operation, structural robustness, and strong torque characteristics across a wide power range. Its engineering philosophy emphasizes mechanical simplicity paired with high fuel efficiency, making it a dependable platform for both prime and standby power applications across emerging and established markets. For GloPower, Baudouin adds a heritage-backed yet technologically current option to the portfolio — giving clients access to time-tested French engineering with the performance characteristics today's power demands require.

Built for Continuous, Connected Power

Generac engineers its natural gas generator sets from the block up, purpose-built for sustained standby and continuous power rather than adapted from automotive platforms. Its gas engines are paired with in-house manufactured alternators and enclosures, delivering consistent performance, reduced noise, and extended runtime without the storage and refuelling demands of liquid fuel. Backed by advanced remote monitoring and diagnostic capability, Generac gensets are engineered for grid-independent reliability. For GloPower, Generac strengthens the gas genset offering with a platform built specifically for clean-burning, low-maintenance power — ideal for clients prioritizing operational continuity and reduced environmental impact.
