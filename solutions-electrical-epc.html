What We Deliver

Electrical infrastructure projects fail at the handoffs — between design and procurement, between installation and commissioning. GloPower's EPC model removes those gaps, delivering substation design, switchgear, critical power systems, and automation integration as one continuous scope, from concept through energization.

HT/LT Electrical panels and Infrastructure

High and low-tension power distribution systems engineered for safe, code-compliant operation across industrial and commercial sites.

Bus Trunking and HT/LT Cabling

Busbar trunking and HT/LT cable systems designed for efficient power distribution across large-scale facilities and multi-level installations.

Substation Design & Installation

End-to-end substation engineering and installation, from layout design through energization.

Transformers & Switchgear Solutions

Transformer and switchgear systems specified and installed to match site load requirements and safety standards.

UPS & Critical Power Systems

Uninterruptible power systems engineered to protect critical loads from outages and power quality issues.

SCADA & Automation Integration

Monitoring and automation systems that give operators real-time visibility and control over electrical infrastructure.

How We Approach
EPC Solution

From Concept to Energization

Large infrastructure projects don't fail on engineering — they fail on coordination. GloPower's EPC process keeps design, procurement, and execution under one accountable scope, so nothing gets lost between handoffs.

Design

Detailed engineering and load analysis aligned to site requirements, codes, and client specifications before procurement begins

Execute

Procurement, installation, and integration carried out as one continuous, accountable scope — not separate contracts

Energize

Commissioning, testing, and handover with full documentation, ready for operational load
